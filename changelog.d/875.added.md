**`explore` and `transform plan` flag reserved-word column names.** `explore profile`, `map` and `relationships` add a data-quality note for a column whose name the connector's dialect reserves (`window` on DuckDB, say), and `transform plan` warns when a `schema.yml` puts tests on such a column without `quote: true`, which dbt needs for those tests to compile. The word lists are sqlglot's, so DuckDB, BigQuery, Redshift and Trino are checked; Postgres, Snowflake, Databricks and ClickHouse are not yet.
