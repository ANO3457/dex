**`maintain check` and `maintain grain` pay BigQuery's 10 MB minimum once per table rather than once per probe.** The drift probes one table needs (key distinct counts, composite and declared grains, declared join arity, the null-fraction survey, and the semantic dimension counts under `check`) now run as one statement per table, so a sweep over many small tables bills a floor per table instead of two to four. The estimate drops the same way, and also stops reserving a profile's escalation queries that the null survey never issues. Findings are unchanged. A block-sampled table keeps its null survey as a separate statement, and a budget that cannot cover the combined statement falls back to the separate probes.
