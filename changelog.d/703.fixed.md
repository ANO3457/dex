**`transform rename` and `transform remove` refuse when a Python model cannot be read or parsed.** An unreadable or unparseable Python model in `model-paths` may contain `dbt.ref(...)` or `dbt.source(...)` calls that dex could not inspect. `transform rename` and `transform remove` now consume the per-kind reference-query limits and refuse operations on models, sources, seeds, and snapshots with the offending file path and reason, while unrelated operations such as `var` renames continue as before.
